About

Henry Lux is a New York–based photographer working across portraiture, still life, and personal photographic projects.

His personal work includes Fatherhood, Observation, and Labor Creates.

Education

He is currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Studio Art at Hunter College, CUNY.

His background includes coursework in photography, studio lighting, and studio art at the School of Visual Arts, LaGuardia Community College, and Hunter College.

Recognition

He received The Arnold & Augusta Newman Foundation Scholarship at LaGuardia Community College.
